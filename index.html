LIVE fifamap.org

FIFAMap

A trip planner for the 2026 World Cup covering all 16 host cities across three countries — match schedules, hotels, transport, and nightlife, with an AI itinerary builder that works in eight languages. Monetized through affiliate travel bookings, a paid planning tier, and a full sponsorship program for venues and brands.

LIVE hopemap.live

HopeMap

A real-time, community-verified map of shelters, food banks, and mental health resources for people in crisis — built on the insight that static directories fail the moment a shelter fills up. Includes an org dashboard for nonprofits, offline mode for prepaid phones, and AI-powered resource matching. Launching in Los Angeles with a phased national rollout plan.

React Native + React PostgreSQL / PostGIS Claude API matching Firebase real-time Product strategy
LIVE thelacityguide.com

The LA City Guide

An independent visitor guide to Los Angeles for the 2028 Olympics — neighborhoods, venue transport, honest dining picks, and an itinerary builder, written for a global audience with country-specific entry and culture notes. Backed by a full advertising rate card, from single listings to six-figure enterprise sponsorships, plus a paid concierge trip-planning service.
