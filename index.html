Five products, all live

Each one solves a specific, unglamorous problem for a specific person, then gets out of their way.

Municipal Watch

municipalwatch.org

Tracks every city council meeting, planning commission agenda and zoning permit in a local business owner's city, then turns the jargon into a plain-English weekly brief. Real-time alerts flag anything that could hit their street or property value.

Built: Stripe subscriptions with tiered pricing, civic data monitoring, plain-language automation

ClearScan

clearscan.org

Radiology reports are written for doctors, not patients. ClearScan translates the findings into language a patient can understand in seconds, a small product aimed squarely at a moment of real anxiety.

Built: patient-facing healthtech UX, plain-language translation

FIFAMap

fifamap.org

A trip planner for World Cup events covering all 16 host cities in three countries: match schedules, hotels, transport and nightlife, with an AI itinerary builder in eight languages. It earns through affiliate bookings, a paid planning tier and a sponsorship program for venues and brands.

Built: AI itinerary planning, eight-language i18n, affiliate revenue, sponsorship tiers, Stripe paywall

HopeMap

hopemap.live

A real-time, community-verified map of shelters, food banks and mental health resources for people in crisis, built because static directories fail the moment a shelter fills up. It has an org dashboard for nonprofits, offline mode for prepaid phones and AI resource matching. Launching in Los Angeles, with a phased national rollout.

Built: React Native and React, PostgreSQL with PostGIS, Claude API matching, Firebase real-time, product strategy

The LA City Guide

thelacityguide.com

An independent visitor guide to Los Angeles for the 2028 Olympics: neighborhoods, venue transport, honest dining picks and an itinerary builder, with country-specific entry and culture notes for a global audience. Backed by an advertising rate card from single listings to six-figure sponsorships, plus a paid concierge trip-planning service.

Built: content strategy, itinerary builder, monetization design, international UX

What five launches teach you

The through-line isn't the industry. It's the habit: find a real problem, ship something that works, and make the money side hold together.

Product and strategy

  • Scoping an idea down to something one person can build and ship
  • Positioning and copy a stranger understands in five seconds
  • Phased go-to-market, from one city to a national rollout

Engineering

  • Full-stack builds across web and React Native
  • Geospatial data with PostgreSQL and PostGIS
  • AI for matching, translation and itinerary generation

Monetization

  • Stripe subscriptions and one-time checkout
  • Affiliate revenue across hotels, flights, tours and tickets
  • Sponsorship and advertising rate cards, built from scratch

Craft

  • Multilingual, accessible interfaces for global audiences
  • Real-time features: live status, alerts, offline mode
  • SEO and metadata done properly, not as an afterthought
