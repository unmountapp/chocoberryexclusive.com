A few of our products

Municipal Watch

LiveCivic tech

City hall decisions hit local businesses without warning. Municipal Watch reads the agendas so owners don't have to.

Case study
  • Any cityin any country
  • $49 and $99monthly Starter and Pro plans
  • 2 weeksfree pilot, no card needed

The problem

Council meetings, planning agendas and zoning permits are public, but buried in jargon across hundreds of pages. Owners, landlords and contractors usually hear about a decision after it's made.

What we built

  • Monitoring of council meetings, planning agendas and permit activity
  • Plain-English weekly briefs written for the business, not the clerk
  • Real-time SMS and email alerts with custom keyword tracking
  • Sample briefs for nine Southern California cities, so buyers see real value first

How it earns

Tiered Stripe subscriptions, with a free two-week pilot that turns curious visitors into paying members.

Visit municipalwatch.org

ClearScan

LiveHealthtech

Patients often read their scan results before a doctor explains them. ClearScan explains them first, in seconds.

Case study
  • 1 steppaste a report, read the answer
  • Secondsfrom jargon to plain language

The problem

Radiology reports are written doctor to doctor. A patient opens their portal, sees "mild bilateral neural foraminal narrowing" and spends the weekend worrying.

What we built

A deliberately small product for an anxious moment: one field, one action, and a calm explanation that tells the patient what the findings mean and what to ask their doctor.

Visit clearscan.org

FIFAMap

LiveSports travel

A trip planner for World Cup events, covering all host cities now and in the future, with an AI itinerary builder in eight languages.

Case study
  • 8 languagesfrom English to Arabic and Japanese
  • Every tournamentmen's, women's, youth and club
  • 3 revenue streamspaid plans, affiliates, sponsors

The problem

Fans following a World Cup juggle match schedules, tickets, hotels, transport and nightlife across many host cities, often in a language that isn't their own.

What we built

  • A tournament hub and archive for every FIFA World Cup, past, present and future
  • Host city guides, stadium schedules, watch parties and nightlife
  • An AI planner that builds day-by-day itineraries in any of eight languages

How it earns

A freemium planner (free messages, then a $2.99 PDF, a $4.99 single trip or a $9.99 monthly Tournament Pass), plus affiliate bookings for hotels, flights, tickets and tours, and a sponsorship program for venues and brands.

Visit fifamap.org

HopeMap

In pilot planningCrisis response

A live map of shelter, food and mental health help that's open right now, starting in Greater Los Angeles.

Case study
  • 4 languagesat launch, with 50 more planned
  • Offlinefor prepaid phones and limited data
  • 3 phasesLA pilots 2027, SoCal 2028, national 2029+

The problem

Static directories go stale the moment a shelter fills up. People in crisis end up calling around, often with little battery and less data.

What we built

  • A community map showing open, limited or closed status, bed counts and wait times
  • AI matching: describe your situation in plain words, get nearby open help
  • A trust system that gives verified reporters more weight
  • A free dashboard for nonprofits, plus one-tap 911, 988 and 211

Built with

React Native (Expo) and React, PostgreSQL with PostGIS, Claude API, Firebase, Redis, AWS and Cloudflare.

Visit hopemap.live

The LA City Guide

LiveTravel media

The practical guide to visiting Los Angeles for the 2028 Olympics, built as a media business from the start.

Case study
  • 35+neighborhood guides
  • 50+restaurant picks
  • $99 to $5,000monthly advertising tiers

The problem

LA is spread out, car-heavy and confusing for first-time visitors, and Olympic venues are scattered across the region. Visitors need practical answers, and local businesses need a way to reach them.

What we built

  • Neighborhood and Olympic venue guides with transport notes
  • An itinerary builder with ready-made 3 to 14 day plans
  • Country-specific entry and culture notes for international visitors
  • Clearly labelled sponsored placements, so readers can trust the picks

How it earns

A full advertising rate card, from $99 listings to venue-zone sponsorships and a $5,000 a month official partner tier, plus newsletter sponsorships and paid custom trip planning from $149.

Visit thelacityguide.com

How we work

A short path from your first email to a product people use. You always know what's being built, why, and what's next.

  1. Talk it through

    Tell us the problem and who it's for. We'll say honestly whether we're the right team for it.

  2. Agree the scope

    What ships first, what it takes, and how the product earns or saves money. Written down before any build starts.

  3. Build and show

    You see working software early and often, not slide decks, and steer it as it takes shape.

  4. Launch and grow

    We take it live with analytics and SEO in place, then keep improving it with you if you want us to.

Make it plain

If a stranger can't understand it in five seconds, it isn't finished. That goes for the interface, the copy and the pricing page.

Ship the real thing

A working product in front of real people beats a perfect plan. We scope small, launch, then improve.

Make the money work

Every product we build has a clear way to earn, whether that's subscriptions, affiliates, sponsors or a paid tier.

Design for the hard moment

Someone anxious, in a hurry, on a cheap phone or reading in their second language. If it works for them, it works for everyone.

Tools we use: React and React Native, PostgreSQL with PostGIS, Firebase, Redis, Stripe, the Claude API, Cloudflare and AWS. We're happy to work in the stack your team already has.
